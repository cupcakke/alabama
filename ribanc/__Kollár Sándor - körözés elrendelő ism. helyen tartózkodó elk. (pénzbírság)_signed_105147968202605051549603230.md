# DECISION

on ordering a search for a person

Pursuant to Section 139(5) and (6) of Act II of 2012 on Administrative Offences, Administrative Offence Procedure and the Administrative Offence Registration System (hereinafter: the Administrative Offences Act), I hereby order a search for the perpetrator **Sándor Kollár** (born: Kiskunfélegyháza, 16 April 2003, mother's name: Katalin Szabó) for the purpose of his transfer to the **Kiskunhalas National Penitentiary Institution (6400 Kiskunhalas, Szegedi út 75.)** in order to execute the administrative offence confinement replacing the fine. The data indicated during the search shall be **kept on record** in the search registration system until withdrawal, but at the longest until **4 May 2027**. For one year calculated from the present decision, however this date may not exceed the absolute two-year limitation period of the enforceability of the punishment. Pursuant to Section 2(1) of Decree No. 21/2023 (VI. 30.) BM of the Minister of the Interior (hereinafter: the BM Decree), in the event of being brought before the authority with the use of a service vehicle – carried out as a result of the ordered search – he is obliged to pay HUF 19,700, and in the event of being brought before the authority on foot, HUF 4,000 per hour in proportion to the number of official persons participating in the measure. Pursuant to Section 2(2) of the BM Decree, in the event of being brought before the authority with the use of a service vehicle – carried out as a result of the ordered search – if the duration of being brought before the authority exceeds 2 hours, from the 3rd hour onwards he is obliged to pay a further HUF 4,000 per hour in proportion to the number of official persons participating in the measure, and if the distance covered by the service vehicle exceeds fifty kilometres, from the fifty-first kilometre onwards a further HUF 100 per kilometre. /in the event of being brought in/*

## There is no remedy against my decision.

# REASONING

I order a search for the person Sándor Kollár for the purpose of his transfer to the Kiskunhalas National Penitentiary Institution (6400 Kiskunhalas, Szegedi út 75.), since he has not paid the fine of HUF 62,400 imposed by decision No. 03805/451-2/2025.szabs. of the Kiskunfélegyháza Police Headquarters, dated 18 August 2025 and which became final on 4 October 2025, nor has he redeemed it by community service. On the basis of order No. T.21.Szpá.12406/2025/4. of the Baja District Court dated 18 December 2025, the perpetrator failed to report on 11 March 2026 at the designated penitentiary institution in order to serve 9 (nine) days of administrative offence confinement due to the non-payment of the imposed administrative offence fine.

The perpetrator did not receive the notice dated 28 January 2026 at his permanent residence at 6100 Kiskunfélegyháza, Szőlő utca 10., door 3., the delivery receipt was returned to the file on 6 February 2026 with the note "moved away".
